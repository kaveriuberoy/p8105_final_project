Interactive 3D Plot

This interactive 3D scatterplot visualizes how county-level violent crime rates, homicide rates, and COVID-19 deaths relate across the United States. Each point represents a county, and the vertical axis and color scale show the intensity of COVID-19 mortality in 2020. This view allows us to observe how violence-related factors correspond with pandemic outcomes when considered simultaneously. The patterns that emerge in the visualization are summarized below.

Key patterns observed in the 3D violence–COVID plot
Key pattern
Higher homicide rates are linked with higher COVID-19 mortality.
Violent crime rate also increases with COVID-19 deaths, but more gradually.
Counties low in both homicide and violent crime tend to have lower COVID-19 mortality.
Looking at all three variables together helps show how violence levels relate to COVID-19 outcomes.
